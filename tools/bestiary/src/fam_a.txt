@family bone | 骸骨 | The Unburnt | global, home C
concept: The dead who were buried whole because there was no fire left to burn them, risen to look for the pyre they were promised.
lore: In 瑟蘭 the dead were always given to the fire and their ash carried up to the Sun-Forge. When the Forge died there was no fuel to waste on corpses, so a thousand years of dead went into the ground whole — 未焚者, the Unburnt. They rise carrying what they were buried with (kettle caps, grave-linen, funeral urns, coffin boards) and walk toward any warmth, because warmth is the pyre they are owed. The Hushed Order's bishops conscript them, but they need no master to envy a living fire. Everything they do is stiff soldier-drill or grave goods used as weapons; nothing is magic. Death: they fall apart joint by joint and the bones finally burn — one quick flare of pale ember, then ash: the cremation they were waiting for.
has: swarmer, melee, ranged, shield-bearer, reviver, bruiser
lacks: caster — bones fight only with what they were buried with; their magic belongs to the Order priests who march with them, so a bone pack is dangerous in proportion to who is leading it.

@family husk | 飢骸 | The Fireless | global, home W
concept: Living people who went too long without ember-light: the warmth left, the hunger stayed.
lore: Husks are not the dead. They are villagers, pilgrims and beacon-keepers whose fire went out and who did not die of it — 失燼, ember-loss. The body cools to the temperature of the dusk, the mind narrows to a single want, and what is left follows warmth the way water runs downhill: lamps, cook-fires, the heat in another person's chest. Families chained them up or locked them out rather than kill them, so husks still wear nightshirts, bindings and tether-chains, and many clutch a dead lantern they cannot put down. They attack with hands and teeth and pile onto anything warm. Death: a husk does not crumble at once — the last of its body heat leaves as a breath of orange sparks, and only then does the grey flesh flake to ash.
has: swarmer, melee (puller), exploder, bruiser (splits), summoner
lacks: ranged and caster — a husk fight is a pure rush from every side; the Coldcaller makes the rush bigger instead of giving it a back line.

@family cult | 寂滅教團 | The Hushed Order | global, home N
concept: The order that calls the dusk a mercy and puts out every fire it finds.
lore: The Order teaches that the Sun-Forge did not fail — it was allowed to rest — and that every lamp lit since is a scream in a house of sleep. Its members take the vow of quiet (lips sewn or masked), carry snuffers instead of torches, and travel with wet shrouds, cold censers and bells whose clappers have been cut out. They are living people, organised and drilled: a complete army with skirmishers, line troops, marksmen, priests, healers and champions, which is why they are the one family that has every role. Their weapons extinguish — snuffer-poles, bolts that eat light, smoke that mends their own. Death: silent; the body folds, the mask falls, and the ash is black with a rim of white sparks — their own fire, finally let out.
has: swarmer, melee, ranged, caster, bruiser, support (shielder), healer, debuffer, stealth pouncer
lacks: nothing — the Order is the game's reference "full army"; every other family is defined by what it is missing compared with this one.

@family wraith | 縛魂 | The Bound | global, home C
concept: Souls that no pyre released, chained to their own shrouds by the Order as wardens and as proof.
lore: A soul was meant to rise with the smoke. With no pyre it stays where the body fell and slowly forgets everything but the cold. The Order finds these and keeps them — a chain twice round the shroud, a seal with the Order's mark — as wardens, and as evidence that rest is kinder than fire. Wraiths are drawn to the ember in the hero the way the Unburnt are, but they cannot hold it: their touch only takes heat. They drift through walls of bodies, blink away from blades, and nest in dead lanterns. Death: the chain drops, the shroud collapses empty, and one small warm spark rises straight up — the only monsters whose ash goes upward.
has: caster (teleporter), melee (phasing), swarmer (lantern wisps)
lacks: bruiser, ranged physical and every kind of support — wraiths are solitary griefs, each alone even in a pack; they never help each other, so the family has no force multiplier and is always a garnish on someone else's pack.

@family reaver | 盜燼眾 | Ember-takers | global, home W
concept: People who stopped tending fire and started stealing it — raiders who sell stolen embers to anyone, the Order included.
lore: Keeping a beacon alive costs a village everything it has. The Ember-takers decided long ago that it is easier to take a fire than to feed one: they break small beacons, strip caravans and carry the stolen embers off in iron cages to sell — to frightened towns at a ruinous price, or to the Order, which pays well for fires to put out. They are ordinary living people, and that is the unsettling part. They smear cold ash on their skin so husks cannot feel their warmth, wear strings of dead lanterns as tallies, and fight with a lamplighter's trade tools turned into weapons: hook-poles, wick-shears, oil pots. No magic at all. Death: they die like people — a real fall, no crumble; only when the stolen ember they carried gutters out does the body ash with it.
has: swarmer, melee (puller), bomber, support (aura), bruiser, handler (with hounds)
lacks: caster and healer — they are thieves, not believers: nothing they own is magic and nobody stops to help anybody.

@family dusk | 灰暮野物 | Dusk-fauna | all regions (regional sets)
concept: The animals that survived a thousand sunless years by learning to hunt heat.
lore: Nothing that needed the sun is left. What remains has spent a thousand years learning one trick: find warmth and take it. Dusk-fauna are pale, large-eared or eyeless, with heat-pits instead of eyes and coats full of ash; they sleep on old hearth-stones and warm brass and follow travellers for the heat of their lamps. They are not evil — they are hungry — and they are the only family that comes to the hero out of appetite rather than belief or grief. Each region has its own beasts. Attacks are bites, charges, swoops and pounces; several steal the hero's resource (the ember) instead of life. Death: an animal death — they drop, kick once, and the ash that takes them is plain grey with no sparks at all.
has: swarmer (pack), charger, bruiser, stealth pouncer, ranged spitter, flyer (thief), flyer (ceiling ambusher), flyer (drain), swarm
lacks: caster, support and reviver — they are animals; nothing in the family thinks about the fight.
