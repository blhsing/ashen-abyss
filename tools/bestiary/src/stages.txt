# Production stages. Row: n | title | what and why
0 | On disk today | The 14 models already built. They cover 15 type ids because the cinderling (`imp`) is `mon_fiend` at 0.6 scale.
1 | Finish the 17, then every variant of an existing base | First the three planned models that are not on disk yet (`mon_mire_hag`, `mon_rotspawn`, `mon_brass_sentinel`). Then all variants whose base body already exists — skeleton, husk, cultist / priest, wraith, frostbound, sand-revenant, fiend — which need new parts on an existing body builder and no new sculpt. This is the cheapest stage per type and it completes the role sets of seven of the eight current families; the cinderling gets its own model here.
2 | Human-sized Rig A bases | Fourteen new sculpts covering every new family that has human-sized members; eight of them unlock the variants of stage 3. After this stage every region has at least one new signature family on screen.
3 | Variants of the stage-2 bases | Fifteen variants (weapon, headgear, proportions, materials on a stage-2 body). Completes the Ember-takers and most role sets of the new Rig A families.
4 | Rig A bruisers and giants | Twelve oversized bases (one bruiser per family that has one), three giant bases, and the three Fuel-bearer variants. Rig A is complete after this stage: 83 types.
5 | Rig F (rigid / floating) | The cheapest new rig — no deformation, at most a hinge, a gimbal or a swarm shader — and its seven creatures touch all seven regions (lantern-ghosts, flame-moths, the three turrets, flickers, the hung bell). Build it first among the new rigs.
6 | Rig B (quadruped) | One skeleton, three body builds (light hound, heavy boar, sprawler). Eleven creatures; gives every region its beasts and the Ash-handler his hounds.
7 | Rig C (arthropod) | One skeleton, four body builds (beetle, spider, scorpion, hermit-crab). Fifteen creatures, the largest pay-off of any new rig: the arthropod members of three families (sexton-beetles, shroud-weavers, brass-hermits) plus three singles.
8 | Rig D (serpentine) | One spine-chain skeleton, two builds (serpent, thick worm). Seven creatures; completes the mire and gives the burrower role its proper bodies.
9 | Rig E (flyer) | One skeleton with three wing builds (feathered, membrane, insect). Six creatures; last because flyers are garnish everywhere and the core of no family.
10 | Named content | Dressing passes on existing bodies for the 28 rare elites, 7 stronghold bosses and 11 of the 14 side-dungeon bosses; three new rigid side-dungeon models (the Walking Sarcophagus, the Workshop Hub, the Great Bellows); four world bosses (one each on rigs C, A, F, D at ×4–5 scale); one new pinnacle model (the First Lamp-keeper) and remix dressings for the seven region bosses.
